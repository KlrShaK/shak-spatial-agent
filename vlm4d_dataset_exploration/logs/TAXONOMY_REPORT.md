# VLM4D question taxonomy

Generated by `python -m vlm4d_dataset_exploration.taxonomy_report` over all 1816 questions (deterministic rules, no sampling, no LLM). Hand-written analysis: `VLM4D_DATASET.md`, `BUCKET_REQUIREMENTS_V5.md`.

## 1. What the dataset ships

| view | source | questions | videos | questions/video | distinct question strings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ego | ego4d | 449 | 300 | 1.50 | 422 |
| exo | davis | 795 | 210 | 3.79 | 661 |
| exo | youtube-vos | 127 | 90 | 1.41 | 95 |
| synthetic | synthetic | 445 | 400 | 1.11 | 210 |

Real ids 1..922 are exo-centric and 923..1371 ego-centric, which is the split the benchmark's `acc_final_statistics.py` hard-codes (id <= 922).

Data quirks an agent has to survive:

- The answer is stored as option TEXT (242 numeric), mapped to a letter by AgentV5's loader; 1 question has two options with the same text (either letter counts).
- 21 questions offer an absence option naming a different object or a typo (e.g. "Is the bike rider spinning clockwise or counter-clockwise in the air?" offers "there are no people in the video"): the existence check must read the option's noun, not assume it is the question's.
- Typos in questions and options are frequent ("bicyclec", "mototrbikes", "clckswise", "plances"); slot validation against the question's words (AgentV5 D3) must tolerate them.

## 4. Sub-task programs
