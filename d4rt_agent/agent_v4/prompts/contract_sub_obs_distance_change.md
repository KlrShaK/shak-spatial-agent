### How to read this measurement

This is the straight-line distance between the observer and the subject,
reported at three moments: the first frame both were measurable, the middle, and
the last. It carries no direction at all — it cannot tell you which way anything
moved, only whether the gap opened or closed.

Distance shrinking means they got closer, whichever of them was moving. A
subject walking toward a fixed camera and a camera pushing toward a fixed
subject produce the same reading, and this question does not distinguish them.

**The noise tolerance is the whole answer for small changes.** A net change at
or below the stated tolerance means the distance did not measurably change —
that is what an option like "Remain unchanged" is for. Do not report a
difference smaller than the tolerance as an increase or a decrease.

Use the overall start-to-end change for the answer. The midpoint is there so you
can see whether the change was steady or reversed direction partway; a distance
that grew and then shrank back may have a small net change even though the
subject moved a great deal.

Choose "Cannot be determined" only if the measurement is reported UNAVAILABLE.
A measured change that is simply small is "unchanged", not "undeterminable".
