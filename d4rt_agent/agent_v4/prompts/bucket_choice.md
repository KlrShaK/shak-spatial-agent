## Step 1 — which measurement decides this question?

Name the one physical quantity this question is asking about, and the frame of
reference it is expressed in. Then choose the bucket that measures it.

Two things to get right:

1. **Whose motion is being asked about** — the subject in the scene, or the
   observer holding the camera? Questions about "the observer" or "the camera"
   are about the camera even when a subject is visible in every frame.
2. **Relative to what** — the mover's own starting position and facing, the
   other party's facing, or nothing at all (a plain distance).

**Ignore the surface wording of the answer options.** The options describe what
an answer could look like, not what is being measured; a question offering
"orbiting clockwise" and "moving right" is still one question about one
quantity. Read the question stem.
