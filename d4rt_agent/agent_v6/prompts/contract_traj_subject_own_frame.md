Forward and right are the subject's own starting axes: forward means where it faced at the stated reference time. Camera motion has been removed. The table lists chronological motion phases in this fixed starting frame.

Positive heading means a turn to the subject's own right; negative heading means a turn to its left. Translation without heading change is a straight path; heading change without translation is turning on the spot. Translation and rotation have separate noise floors. A change at or below its floor is not measured motion, and a small net displacement does not erase an out-and-back path.

Facing can be measured over a shorter span than position. A heading reported for part of the clip describes that span only. Missing facing leaves world- and camera-frame translation available. When reporting where another entity is relative to this subject, its facing at each moment defines its sides.
